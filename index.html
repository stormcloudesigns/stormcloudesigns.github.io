Filament

  • PLA ($20/kg)

    • Very popular filament. It is very stiff and works great for most parts. It cannot withstand heat, enviromental conditions, and is not very durable. Exellent print quality/finish.

  • PETG ($25/kg)

    • Nice middle-ground filament. It can withstand more heat than PLA. Pretty stiff but will flex instead of fracturing under load, increasing durability. Good print quality/finish

  • ABS ($25/kg)

    • High-tempature engineering filament. It is great for outdoor and enviromental conditions. Good print quality/finish.

  • TPU ($35/kg)

    • Flexible rubber-like filament. Good for joints or any other rubber application. Okay print quality/finish.

  • PA6-CF ($50/kg)

    • Extreme engineering grade filament. Very stiff and durable. Due to the state that this filament needs to be printed in, poor print quality and tolarances is expected.
